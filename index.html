Frequently Asked Questions

Is SC Ship Database free?

Yes, the app is completely free to download and use. A voluntary "Support Dev" option is available inside the app if you'd like to help keep it updated.

Is the ship data up to date?

We aim to keep ship stats current with the latest Star Citizen alpha patches. The app displays the current game version on the Ship Database screen.

Is this an official Cloud Imperium Games app?

No. SC Ship Database is an unofficial community fan project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Cloud Imperium Games or Roberts Space Industries.

My data (favorites, loadouts) was lost after an update.

Favorites and saved loadouts are stored locally on your device. Reinstalling the app or restoring from a backup may affect this data. We're working on cloud sync for a future update.

How do I request a feature or report incorrect data?

Email us at walterbarrjr@gmail.com with details and we'll look into it.
